Frequently Asked Questions

NewMUN is a Model United Nations conference organized by the New Indian School.

To participate in NewMUN, you can register as a delegate, chairperson, or part of the press team.

Participating in NewMUN offers numerous benefits, including the development of diplomatic and critical thinking skills, exposure to global issues, and the opportunity to network with like-minded individuals.

The registration process for NewMUN is straightforward. You can register online through our website, and you will receive a confirmation email with further instructions.
